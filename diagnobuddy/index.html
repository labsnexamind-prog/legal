1. Overview

DiagnoBuddy ("the App") is a medical information companion that helps users understand their symptoms using AI-powered analysis. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you register or sign in, we collect:

  • Email address — if you use Email/Password sign-in
  • Name and profile photo — if you use Google Sign-In, sourced from your Google account

2.2 Health Information You Provide

  • Symptoms you describe in the App
  • Follow-up answers you provide during a symptom check
  • Diagnosis history (stored so you can review past checks)

This data is stored in Firebase Firestore, linked to your account, and used solely to provide the App's features. It is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

2.3 Location Information

With your permission, the App accesses your approximate location (coarse location) to display local weather on the home screen. Location data is not stored on our servers.

2.4 Usage Data

Firebase may collect anonymised crash reports and performance data automatically. No personally identifiable usage data is collected beyond what is described above.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, database: Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI — AI symptom analysis (symptoms sent to OpenAI's API): Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — Optional social login: Privacy Policy
  • OpenWeatherMap — Local weather data: Privacy Policy

Symptoms submitted for analysis are processed by OpenAI. We do not send personally identifiable information (name, email) to OpenAI.

5. Medical Disclaimer

DiagnoBuddy provides general health information only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns. Do not delay seeking professional advice based on information from this App.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Account data and history are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), protected by Firebase's security rules.
  • Passwords are managed entirely by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in plain text.
  • We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

  • Your account and history data are retained as long as your account is active.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at jyothishn@gmail.com.
  • We will respond to deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

DiagnoBuddy is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at jyothishn@gmail.com.
